Welcome to AnemiaSense

Hemoglobin is the iron-rich protein in red blood cells that carries oxygen. Anemia occurs when hemoglobin levels are too low.

About Anemia

  • Iron or vitamin B12 deficiency can contribute to anemia.
  • Fatigue and weakness may be symptoms.
  • Pale skin and shortness of breath may also occur.
